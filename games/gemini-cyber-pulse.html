What this atlas includes

This version is designed to work fully offline. It includes a generalized country outline based on Natural Earth at 1:110 million scale; embedded coordinates for all 50 provincial capitals and both autonomous cities; selected cities, rivers, mountain systems, peaks, water bodies, landmarks, and reference corridors.

Accuracy note: the country outlines are generalized for national-scale use. The relief contours are illustrative, river paths are simplified references, and highway/AVE corridors connect selected places schematically. Exact official autonomous-community and province polygons, a complete road network, high-resolution elevation data, street addresses, and satellite imagery are not bundled, so the map does not claim survey-grade or street-level detail. No remote tiles or online geocoder are used.

Base country outlines: Natural Earth, 1:110m cultural vectors (public domain). All interface graphics are generated natively in SVG/CSS. City coordinates are approximate reference coordinates suitable for an overview, not navigation.

Saved map views are stored only in this browser on this device. They work offline and are not uploaded anywhere.
